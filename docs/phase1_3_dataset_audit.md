# PhysioTwin Phase 1.3: Dataset And Source-Access Audit

**Audit date:** 2026-10-15. This is an evidence/access review, not an implementation or data analysis. No human measurements were downloaded, joined, or estimated for this phase.

## Finding

No dataset verified in this audit provides a sufficiently matched set of individual-level, time-aligned glucose measurements and meal appearance, endogenous glucose production, disposal, and (where relevant) urinary glucose loss in the same subjects and experiment. The repository contains no raw physiological dataset. The only time series is a synthetic output generated from hypothetical parameters. The public GEO accession GSE153792 provides transcriptomic samples, not a meal-flux record. Published tracer/model studies establish that suitable measurements and methods have existed, but the checked article records do not expose participant-level time-series data or the complete materials needed to reconstruct their balances.

Therefore, **a complete measured-flux glucose-pool balance cannot currently be constructed from verified accessible data**. A residual calculation could be defined prospectively, but it must not be called measured peripheral disposal unless disposal is independently measured/estimated and the other relevant boundary fluxes are handled.

### Access And Documentation Limits

- For GSE153792, the NCBI E-utilities DataSets summary was retrievable and reported accession, assay class, 34 samples, SRA link and publication PMID. The browser GEO page and direct FTP retrieval were blocked in this audit environment (browser challenge and local TLS certificate-chain failure respectively); no certificate checks were bypassed and no files were saved. Claims are limited to that authoritative summary, not a local inspection of the expression matrix.
- For R1, R2, R6 and R7, the cited abstracts and Europe PMC metadata support the publication facts above. The R2 audit in `docs/literature_audit.md` records publisher full-text/supplement routes as restricted/not exposed and Europe PMC `hasData=N`, `hasSuppl=N`. R1 and R6 are not open access in the checked Europe PMC records and have no linked supplement. No checked record provided individual-level time series for PhysioTwin.
- Publication access restrictions do not prove that data do not exist or cannot be obtained from investigators. They mean the data are not verified as accessible here. Author/institutional requests would need data-use, consent, and governance review.

## Flux Availability Summary

| Quantity | Directly observed in a suitable accessible dataset? | Status in the candidate evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Plasma glucose concentration $C_G(t)$ | **No matched meal/tracer time series located.** GSE153792 is RNA-seq; PhysioTwin output is synthetic. | Direct assay quantity in a future study, but no accessible aligned record verified. |
| Effective glucose-pool amount $M_G(t)$ | No. | Derived as $V_{G,eff}C_G(t)$ only after defining and supporting an effective distribution volume and pool boundary. Concentration is not mass. |
| Meal-derived appearance $R_{a,m}(t)$ | No accessible subject-level flux file located. | Tracer-derived estimate in R1/R6/R2 publication descriptions; not a direct assay reading. |
| Endogenous glucose production $HGP(t)$ | No accessible subject-level flux file located. | Tracer-derived/model-estimated in the cited studies, with method error; not a direct concentration measurement. |
| Peripheral glucose disposal $R_d(t)$ | No accessible matched record located. | May be tracer/model-estimated in a particular protocol or calculated as a residual. A residual is not an independent observation and is not uniquely disposal if other losses/sources are unmeasured. |
| Renal glucose loss $R_{renal}(t)$ | No matched timed-urine data located. | Requires timed urine glucose concentration and volume (or a validated equivalent), actual collection interval and recovery information; zero cannot be assumed outside a stated justified domain. |
| Insulin secretion $S_\beta(t)$ | No matched public subject-level C-peptide series located. | Derived from C-peptide via a declared kinetic/deconvolution method; C-peptide concentration is not secretion rate. |
| Insulin extraction/clearance | No matched kinetic observations located. | Unobserved in ordinary plasma insulin samples; need an explicit kinetic design/model and supporting observations. Not required for a glucose-only flux accounting identity. |

## Audit Conclusion

The answer to “Can we construct the complete balance from accessible matched data now?” is **no**. The existing GSE accession is the only actual data-series record located, and its measurements are transcriptomic. The R1/R2/R6 publications document relevant flux-rich experiments, but their subject-level data, exact protocols and/or full source materials are not available through the checked records. Dataset access and protocol feasibility remain open questions, not evidence that the required data are on hand.
