# Phase 1 Illustrative Experiment

Configuration `experiments/phase1_initial/config.v1.json` defines a deterministic, hypothetical meal-input scenario for software verification. The recorded trajectories are generated by the three-state reduced-order balances and the explicitly illustrative closures in `phase1_model_closures.md`. No values were fitted to clinical observations. The scientific references support measurement/modeling of the named fluxes, but no configured numeric parameter has a traceable published source; the scenario values are hypothetical.

The run can demonstrate that the implemented equations execute reproducibly, preserve their declared pool balances to numerical integration accuracy, and produce finite nonnegative trajectories for this configured case. It cannot establish that the selected closures describe human physiology, identify parameters, predict any patient, validate a meal absorption mechanism, or support clinical decisions. The glucose balance is limited to the modeled systemic pool and excludes renal loss, tissue storage/oxidation, and hepatic precursor pools.

The next scientific decision is whether the gamma-shaped meal forcing, reciprocal-hinge hepatic response, and thresholded-affine beta-cell secretion are acceptable even as illustrative closures, or whether they should remain externally supplied pending protocol/data review. Parameter identifiability and the intended glucose domain must also be settled before calibration or physiological validation.
